Prices were taken from search results between 2026-09-30 and 2026-10-06. **Stock, shipping to Boston and

## Counter-height tables — target spec

42–55" long (ideal 48), 20–24" deep (ideal 24), 34–36" high, 4 legs or panel ends, apron ≤ 3".

### Top picks
- **Linon Claridge** — 47.25 × 23.75 × 36, solid rubberwood, ~$170. Closest to ideal.
- **Linon Concord (black)** — same size, $149. Best under $150.
- Of the three tables originally considered, the **Bike table (17 Stories, $77.99)** rotated 90° was the best fit
  (right height for 24" stools, cheapest), with the Rectangle (Ophelia & Co., $118.99) as runner-up. Skip the
  35.4" Square.
