# Allston living room project

Before doing anything, read `HANDOFF.md` in this folder. It has the full context, decisions already made,
and the next steps. Then use `room-notes.md` and `shopping-lists.md` as the source of truth.

- Units are inches. Origin is the top-left floor corner (kitchen side); x 0–137 toward the TV wall, y 0–155 toward the hallway.
- The 3D model is `living-room-3d/index.html` (single file, three.js r128 from cdnjs + OrbitControls from jsdelivr).
- `tools/walkway_calc.py` reproduces the walkway numbers.
- Do not read or search the person's email. Ask before using any account they haven't pointed you to.
- Keep answers short and give real product links with sizes and prices.
